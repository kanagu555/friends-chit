## 🎛️ **Admin Workflow**

### **1. Add Participants for Monthly Auction**
1. **Go to Participants tab**
2. **Click "Add Participants"**
3. **Select members** using checkboxes (only names shown)
4. **Add multiple at once** for the month's auction
5. **Participants appear** in the list as "Active"

### **2. Conduct Monthly Auction**
1. **Go to Draws tab**
2. **Click "Conduct Month X Auction"**
3. **See eligible participants** (active members who haven't won)
4. **Select winner** by clicking on their name
5. **Click "Declare Winner"** to finalize

### **3. Winner Management**
- **Crown Icon**: Appears next to winner's name
- **Status Update**: Automatically changes to "winner"
- **Future Exclusion**: Winners won't appear in future auctions
- **Visual Indicators**: Clear winner identification

## 🔄 **Monthly Auction Workflow**

### **Before Auction:**
1. **Collect Payments**: Ensure all participants paid ₹10,000
2. **Add Participants**: Select who's participating this month
3. **Verify Eligibility**: Check active status

### **During Auction:**
1. **Open Auction Modal**: Click "Conduct Month X Auction"
2. **Review Participants**: See eligible members
3. **Conduct Shake/Draw**: Admin decision process
4. **Select Winner**: Click on winner's name
5. **Declare Winner**: Finalize the selection

### **After Auction:**
1. **Winner Marked**: Crown icon appears
2. **Payout Calculation**: Based on month (₹95,500 for Month 1)
3. **Status Update**: Winner excluded from future auctions
4. **Next Month Prep**: Ready for next month's process
