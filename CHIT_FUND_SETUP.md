## 🔧 **How to Use**

### **Adding Participants:**
1. Go to **Chit Fund > Participants** tab
2. Click **"Add Participant"**
3. Select from existing members or add new ones
4. Participant automatically joins the current chit

### **Conducting Draws:**
1. Go to **Chit Fund > Draws** tab
2. Click **"Conduct Draw"** for pending month
3. Select winner from eligible participants
4. System automatically calculates payout based on month
5. Record draw results

### **Recording Payments:**
1. Go to **Chit Fund > Payments** tab
2. Click **"Record Payment"**
3. Select participant and month
4. Mark payment as received
5. Track pending payments
